Update from Petrell
24.05.2007
In English this time. - Sally, just for you! The last few days have been wet wet wet. Sea spray and rain, squalls and wind. Last night on my watch, a squall came through with heavy rain, and all of a sudden the whole surface around Petrell was lit up. A little scary at first, until I realized it was the phosphoresence, obviously scared too. A beautiful sight, when I knew what I was looking at :) The radio weather guru "Herb", is guiding us in between highs and lows, so hopefully we are through the worst part by now. We are almost half way to the Azores, with a little more than 1000 miles to go. Do they have ice cream in the Azores?
